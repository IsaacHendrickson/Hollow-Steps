# Modules

Full description of every module, moved unchanged from AGENTS.md (which keeps one line per module).

Gameplay modules (pure rules, no rendering):

- `src/player.rs`: player input, movement rules (including dash),
  collision resolution, and player-focused tests.
- `src/health.rs`: player health, damage, and invincibility; `Health::new(max, invincibility_seconds)` (the player's `max_health` and `invincibility_seconds` from `player.ron`), `set_limits` (a raised max adds the same health, a lowered one cuts the excess, nobody is revived); `MAX_HEALTH` and `INVINCIBILITY_SECONDS` are the shipped values `Health::default()` uses.
- `src/enemy.rs`: enemy behavior, health, knockback, the dying state, and body instances. Its sub-modules: `catalog.rs` (`EnemyDefinitions`, the validated catalog enemies are spawned from), `definition.rs` (`EnemyDefinition`, including `death_secs`, its default and range), `death.rs` (`DeathTimer`, the fade clock of a defeated enemy), `movement.rs` (`Movement`: `Ground` or `Flying` with hover height and sine bob, its validation and the altitude math; a flier ignores gravity, floor snapping and terrain) and `flap.rs` (`Flap` and `FlapClock`: a flier's optional wing flap between two named poses, faster with horizontal speed; behavior poses and the hurt pose are never overridden).
- `src/pose.rs`: semantic pose assets, presentation playback, and validation (enemy and boss pose names).
- `src/body.rs` (+ `body/`): validated RON body templates, local point springs/idle targets, palettes, and pure transforms. The parent re-exports every public item, so `hollow_steps::body::*` paths are unchanged. `template.rs`: `BodyTemplate`, `BodyPart`, `Follow`, `IdleMotion`, `load_body_templates`, motion defaults; `visual.rs`: `BodyVisual`; `instance.rs`: `BodyInstance` (springs, poses, hurt flash); `geometry.rs`: `PartTransform`, `resolve_point`, `resolve_part`.
- `src/body_debug.rs`: pure body overlay primitives (`body_primitives_in` places points in the visual box and the hitbox rectangle in the hitbox), display constants, and nearest-entity selection.
- `src/visual_box.rs`: the drawn box of an enemy or boss apart from its hitbox: `visual_size` validation (1 to 2000 px), `size`, `center` (same horizontal center and bottom edge as the hitbox) and the editor's `with_separate` switch. Gameplay only ever reads the hitbox `size`.
- `src/action_runtime.rs`: shared action execution state, motion, attack output, and actor adapters; `ActionRuntime::execute` is a dispatcher that sends each action to its own function in `src/action_runtime/`: `attacks.rs` (area_blast, shoot_projectile, slam_down, jump_to, lunge), `movement.rs` (wait, hover, walking, patrol, jump_to_player, legacy_slam_down, shared gravity and flight helpers), `blasts.rs` (legacy_area_blast, spawn_rings, explode_rings), `instant.rs` (glides, damage_target, spawn_shockwave, summon), `poses.rs` (pose steps around each action, and the zero-time `pose` action). Executors read the action by reference; never clone it per frame.
- `src/assets.rs`: assets folder resolution (`HOLLOW_STEPS_ASSETS`, next to the executable, then the source tree), load-failure text, and `read_override` (an optional whole-file override of an embedded `data/*.ron` default).
- `src/behavior.rs`: root of the data-driven action sequences, state transitions and RON validation; it only declares modules and re-exports the public API. `src/behavior/`: `action.rs` (the `actions!` table: `Action` enum, loading, editor catalog), `action_serde.rs` (deserialization helpers and older-form readers), `action_info.rs` (wind-up, feedback, attack preview, pose bindings), `condition.rs` (the `conditions!` table and evaluation), `definition.rs` (states, transitions, validation, loading), `runtime.rs` (`Behavior` and `update_with_player_size`), `validators.rs` (shared value checks).
- `src/enemy_attack.rs` (+ `enemy_attack/`): shared attack geometry, timed enemy attacks, and projectiles. The parent re-exports every item at its old visibility, so `hollow_steps::enemy_attack::*` paths are unchanged. `attack_box.rs`: `AttackShape`, `AttackBox`, `DamageHit`, `active_window`; `runtime.rs`: `AttackRuntime` (lunge, jump, slam), motion defaults, `parabolic_position`; `projectile.rs`: `Projectile`; `options.rs`: `Aim`, `AttackSize`, `AttackVector`, `OptionalAttack`, `AttackFeedback`, `AttackWarning`, `spread_angles` and their validators.
- `src/preset.rs`: validated RON behavior preset catalog.
- `src/action_parameters.rs`: labeled scalar editor fields for the shared action registry.
- `src/behavior_values.rs`: numeric behavior controls and transactional validated edits.
- `src/boss_definition.rs`: validated RON boss definitions (optional `body`, checked against body templates and poses) and attack selection settings.
- `src/boss_editor.rs`: pure boss property buffers and ordered attack/action edits.
- `src/boss_special.rs`: boss sequence cursors, selection, actor effect integration, and coin drops.
- `src/boss_special/body_link.rs`: how a boss drives its optional body (`BossBody`): hurt flash, warning tint, facing, death fade, stepping and the pose steps of its attack actions (as `Enemy` applies them); the hitbox stays the definition's `size`.
- `src/combat.rs`: melee swings (forward, up, down), per-direction hitboxes from `StrikeConfig`, knockback direction, once-per-swing pogo, and `MeleeConfig` (sword damage, swing time and cooldown from `player.ron`; `Attack::update_timed` uses them, `update` / `update_directed` use the shipped `SWING_SECONDS` and `ATTACK_COOLDOWN`).
- `src/damage.rs`: `DamageResolver` combines enemy contact/blast/attack and boss action/contact damage into the single hit applied to the player.
- `src/events.rs`: `GameEvent` (jump, landing, dash, swing, enemy hit/death, player damage, coin, item, room, boss) and the `EventQueue` drained by `GameState::take_events`, or by `take_located_events` with the world position each event happened at (`LocatedEvent`); a `PlayerDamaged` event also carries the `HealthLoss` (health lost, health left) in `LocatedEvent::loss`.
- `src/sound.rs`: validated event-to-sound cues (`data/audio.ron`, an `assets/audio.ron` replaces it whole), sound-name and volume checks, sound-file lookup in `assets/audio/` (wav, then ogg), the WAV/Ogg header check, and the per-frame play plan; sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/particles.rs`: validated burst presets (`data/particles.ron`, an `assets/particles.ron` replaces it whole), a seeded deterministic generator, the capped live-particle list, and the event-to-burst rules; the `hud_damage` preset lists no events and is spawned only by `spawn_hud_damage` (the HUD's emitter); sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/feel.rs`: hit-stop and screen shake driven by `GameEvent`s (sine shake, no randomness); tunables in `data/feel.ron` (an `assets/feel.ron` replaces it whole).
- `src/background.rs`: parallax background layers (procedural `Rect`/`Circle`/`Triangle` shapes with a depth factor, per room in `layout.background`), their validation, and the pure `parallax_offset`; sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/postfx.rs`: post-process settings (vignette and glow, `data/postfx.ron`, an `assets/postfx.ron` replaces it whole, `enabled` is the data toggle), the shader source, and the pure `vignette_factor` / `glow_weight` formulas the shader runs; sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/player_body.rs`: the player's look (`data/player_body.ron`, an `assets/player_body.ron` replaces it whole), pose choice from player state, and the `BodyInstance` that plays the `wanderer_*` poses; no template in the assets keeps the box.
- `src/item.rs`: world items and pickup (`within_reach`, the pickup rule items and charms share); `TakenItems` remembers items taken in the run (room, kind, position) and `apply_to_rebuilt` removes them from a room rebuilt from its file, forgetting entries whose item was moved or deleted.
- `src/coin.rs`: collectible coins.
- `src/door_exit.rs`: door exits. `Exit::door` is a free rectangle in room coordinates; `ExitPlacement` is written by hand (a door holds `door` and no edge fields, an edge exit is written as before), `check_door` validates, `RoomDraft::add_door` / `resize_door`, `begin_link_new_door`. See `docs/door-exits.md`.
- `src/room.rs`: rooms (including each room's `terrain_style` and the `charms` lying in it), exits, and room transitions.
- `src/terrain.rs`: solid rectangles, axis-separated substepped collision, and terrain validation.
- `src/terrain_style.rs`: the procedural look of the solids as a draw list of simple shapes (drawing only, never collision): per-solid fill with a lighter top and darker bottom band, speckles and cracks, and along *exposed* edges an outline, a lit top with a jittered stone lip, moss and stalactites; everything seeded from room id plus the solid's position and the palette's `seed_offset` (`particles::Rng`, no clock); `TerrainStyle` is the resolved palette (fill, edge, highlight and accent colors 0 to 1, seed offset, moss on/off; default = the original look, validated); a room's `layout.terrain_style` is a `RoomStyle` (see `terrain_styles.rs`); `build_room` draws a whole room from `RoomInputs` (bounds, floor, exits, solids) and a `Look` (palette, speckle and crack density, boundary thickness, boundary on/off): the solids plus, when on, the visual-only boundary slabs (ceiling, walls, floor slab, doorways cut at exits) through the same code; `TerrainLook` caches the list (`shapes` for the solids alone, `room_shapes` for a whole room, which also says whether the boundary is in it) until its inputs change. Design note: `docs/terrain-look.md`. Sub-modules `edges.rs` (`exposure`: which stretches of each edge are open, for touching, stacked and overlapping solids), `colors.rs`, `rock.rs` (the shapes of one solid), `boundary.rs` (`boundary_slabs`: the slab rectangles and their doorways); tests in `tests.rs`, `limit_tests.rs`, `edge_tests.rs`, `boundary_tests.rs` and `room_tests.rs`.
- `src/terrain_styles.rs`: the terrain look presets and the per-room style: `TerrainPreset` and the `TerrainStyles` catalog (`data/terrain_styles.ron`, an `assets/terrain_styles.ron` replaces it whole; `stone` is the default and the original look), validation (unique names, at least one preset, finite numbers, colors 0 to 1, boundary thickness at most 512, density at most 10), and `RoomStyle`, the type of `layout.terrain_style` (preset name, optional color and moss overrides, seed offset, `draw_boundary`; every field optional, bare values or `Some(..)`, the pre-preset palette format loads as overrides, only differences are written). `TerrainStyles::resolve` turns a `RoomStyle` into a `terrain_style::Look` (unknown preset is an error listing the known ones; overrides beat the preset). Tests in `terrain_styles/tests.rs` (presets, catalog, loading) and `terrain_styles/style_tests.rs` (`RoomStyle`); the game side is `src/game/terrain_link.rs`.
- `src/game/terrain_link.rs`: loads the terrain presets with the rooms (every room must resolve against them, so a room naming an unknown preset stops the game from loading or reloading) and the `GameState::terrain_styles` accessor the front end resolves each room's look from.
- `src/camera.rs`: follow camera with look-ahead and room clamping, screen-shake view offset, letterbox fit, HUD anchors; tunables in `data/camera.ron`. `src/game/camera_bounds.rs`: `GameState::camera_bounds`, the rectangle the camera is clamped to (the room bounds plus the boundary wall thickness).
- `src/timestep.rs`: fixed 120 Hz step accumulator (capped) and edge-input latch.
- `src/pause_menu.rs`: the pause menu as pure state: `PauseMenu` (closed, or open on a `PauseScreen`: `Main` with Unpause / Settings / Exit, or `Settings`: a `Bind(Action)` row per action, Reset keybinds and Back), `escape` (also cancels a wait for a key), `step`, `step_slot` (the highlighted primary or alternate key), `select`, `select_slot`, `activate` / `activate_selected` / `activate_slot` (a keybind row starts waiting for a key: `listening`; returns `PauseOutcome::Exit` when the program should end, `ResetKeybinds` for Reset keybinds and `AbandonRun` for the confirmed Abandon run, which `GameState` does itself); during a run (`set_in_run`) the main screen is `RunMain` with Abandon run between Settings and Exit, which opens the `Confirm` screen (Abandon run / Keep playing, Keep playing selected, Escape back) and the one-line `note`. The selection is locked while waiting for a key. Sub-module `layout.rs`: the layout the drawing and the mouse hit test share (`panel_rect` per screen, `button_rects`, `item_at`, `slot_rects`, `slot_at`, `status_rect`). Tests in `src/pause_menu/tests.rs` and `src/pause_menu/run_tests.rs` (the run screens).
- `src/keybinds.rs`: rebindable controls as pure state (design note `docs/keybinds.md`): `Action`, `Slot` (primary, alternate), `Keybinds` (shipped keys in `Default`, `rebind` (a key in use trades places, an empty alternate cannot take a primary key), `clear` (alternate only), `reset`, `check`, `triggered`), `Rebound` / `RebindError`. Sub-modules: `keys.rs` (`BINDABLE`, the only keys that can be bound and the one name each has, `first_pressed`), `store.rs` (the `keybinds.ron` format, `load` / `save` (staged) / `remove`, `config_dir` per platform and `default_path`), `input.rs` (`player_input` from held and pressed key tests to `PlayerInput`, `hint_line` for the HUD), `notes.rs` (the message line's texts, `key_label`). Tests in `src/keybinds/tests.rs` and `src/keybinds/store_tests.rs`.
- `src/tuning.rs`: player (movement, jump, dash, size, max health, immunity), melee (sword damage, swing time, cooldown), strike (hitbox sizes, pogo speed) and enemy-knockback tunables from `data/player.ron` (the numbers added later are `#[serde(default)]` so older files load) (an `assets/player.ron` replaces it whole), validation, staged save.
- `src/tuning_fields.rs`: the editable `player.ron` numbers (`Field`, `FieldSpec`): one range per field shared by validation and the F4 window; re-exported from `tuning`.
- `src/player_editor.rs`: pure `PlayerDraft` for editing `player.ron` and `vitals.ron` in the editor (saved, edited and dirty copies of the `TuningConfig` and of the `VitalsConfig`: `is_dirty` for either, `is_stats_dirty` / `is_vitals_dirty`, `set_vitals_field`, `reset_vitals_field`, `save_vitals`; `save` writes `player.ron` only, per-field and per-section reset, `set_field` against the shared `Field` table, the cross-field jump rule, the size-against-room-spawn check over `SpawnCheck`s, and `save`, which validates first and writes nothing when invalid); `EditorSession::spawn_checks` lists every room with the draft substituted. The vitals file has one rule across files: `CharmCost` (the cost of each charm in the charm list that is saved with it), `vitals_problem`, `validate_vitals` and `save_vitals_checked` refuse a max mana below the dearest charm and write nothing (`save_vitals` is the bare write); `GameState::check_vitals_save` asks it before F4 or the Player panel writes anything, and `editor_guard::save_all` asks it with the list it is about to write.
- `src/player_history.rs`: the Player panel's own undo history, apart from the room's `editor_history`: `PlayerSnapshot` (stats, vitals, look and the body template of one moment, restored with `restore`), `PlayerHistory` (`observe` turns a whole drag into one step, `record`, `undo`, `redo`, a 100-step limit); the keys reach it only while the Player panel has focus.
- `src/player_look_editor.rs`: pure `PlayerLookDraft` for editing `player_body.ron` in the editor (saved and edited `PlayerLook`, dirty, reset, `problem` and `check_savable` against the editor's templates and poses, a staged `save` that refuses a look naming a template or pose that is not on disk), `preview_body` (a body at rest in a chosen pose), and `EditorSession::player_preview`, the player at the room's spawn point from the unsaved look and size that the canvas draws.
- `src/charms.rs`: charms as pure rules: `Charm` and `Effect` (`MaxHealth`, `MoveSpeedPercent`, `SwordDamage`) from `data/charms.ron` (an `assets/charms.ron` replaces it whole), `CharmCatalog` (validation: unique id, unique name ignoring case, cost from 0 to the base max mana, ranges, color), `CharmState` (owned and equipped ids, `can_equip`, `equip`, `unequip`, `toggle` -> `Toggle`, `max_mana`) and `effective` (base `TuningConfig` and `VitalsConfig` plus the equipped charms; never changes the base); `Charm` and `Effect` also serialize, `CharmCatalog::new` validates a list, and `CharmState::grant`, `grant_starting` and `trim_to_budget` are what finding a charm, a test run and a saved list use. Sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/vitals.rs`: pure player vitals (health, trailing bar, mana, hold-to-heal) driven by the tick, with `set_max_health` for a changed `player.max_health`; no window, no `GameState`. Tunables in `data/vitals.ron` (an `assets/vitals.ron` replaces it whole); `VitalsField`/`FieldSpec` ranges shared by validation and the F4 window. Tests in `src/vitals/tests.rs`.
- `src/editor.rs`: pure room editing, validation, serialization, and file save boundary (`RoomLayout::charms`, `Placement::Charm`; re-exports `editor_files`).
- `src/editor_files.rs`: room file names, the staged room save and loading the rooms beyond the three built in (`room_filename`, `save_room`, `load_extra_rooms`), moved out of `editor.rs`, which re-exports them. A `room_*.ron` whose number is not a plain number without leading zeros stays an error; its message names the file and says to rename or move it.
- `src/room_charms.rs`: charms lying in rooms: `CharmPlacement` (charm id and position in a room file), `WorldCharm` and `LyingCharm`, `CHARM_SIZE`, the pickup rule through `item::within_reach`, and `RoomLayout`'s charm checks (position inside the room and outside the solids, `validate_charm_ids`). Sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/editor_charms.rs`: `EditorSession::placement_catalog` (the definitions' list plus "Charm: <name>" from the Charms panel's working list) and `charm_swatch`.
- `src/charm_editor.rs`: pure `CharmDraft` for editing `charms.ron` in the editor (saved list, edited list, dirty; the field table `FIELDS`, `MANA_COST`, `AMOUNTS` and `EffectKind`; per-field and cross-field `problems` (unique id and name, cost against the saved base max mana, amount range per kind); `new_charm`, `delete`, `reset_charm`, `reset_all`; a staged `save` that validates first and writes nothing when invalid). Sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/charm_history.rs`: the Charms panel's own undo and redo (`CharmHistory`: snapshots of the edited list, one step per drag, `observe`, `undo`, `redo`). Sub-module `tests.rs`.
- `src/editor_session.rs`: `EditorSession`, the open editor session (draft, definitions, room catalog, terrain look presets, unsaved flag, the `parked` drafts of rooms an exit link touched, and `RoomWork`, one room's draft with its path and new / unsaved flags), moved out of `editor.rs` (which re-exports it as `editor::EditorSession`).
- `src/editor_look.rs`: pure terrain look edits on the room draft (`set_look_preset`, `set_look_color` / `reset_look_color`, `set_look_moss`, `set_draw_boundary`, `set_seed_offset`, `reroll_seed`, checked against the preset catalog; a color or moss equal to the preset's is no override), `EditorSession::edit_look` (marks the session unsaved only when the style changed) and `LookPreview`, the cached draw list the editor canvas draws (same inputs and room id as the game). Tests in `tests/editor_look.rs`.
- `src/editor_guard.rs`: unsaved-changes state machine (Save / Discard / Cancel) and the approved-action runner (`GuardedAction::NewNamedRoom` starts a room with the name the New room window gave); `unsaved_besides_rooms` names what the room list does not ("Player", "Charms") and `request_for` opens the dialog with it, `save_all` saves the charms, the rooms and then the player (the room before the charms when the list deletes a charm a saved room places), and `perform` drops the player's and the charms' unsaved edits (`discard_player_edits`, `discard_charm_edits`) before a Discard.
- `src/room_name.rs`: room names: the format rule (trimmed, 1-32 letters, digits, space, `-`, `_`), case-insensitive uniqueness against every other room and unsaved draft, display names for unnamed rooms (`First`, `Second`, `Boss`, `Room N`), the default "Room N" for a new room, `room_title`, and `NamePrompt`, the text and error behind the New room window.
- `src/editor_name.rs`: the room name in `EditorSession`: `set_room_name_text` (good text goes into the draft, bad text waits in `name_input` with `name_error`), `check_room_name`, `suggested_room_name`, `room_labels` / `title_of` / `room_title`, and `check_name_for_save`.
- `src/exit_link.rs`: the exit-link flow as a small pure state machine (`ExitLink`, `Step::{Choosing, Naming, Connecting}`) with its transitions as `EditorSession` methods (`begin_link`, `begin_link_new_exit`, `link_choose`, `link_new_room`, `link_name_room`, `link_place_exit`, `link_arrival` / `link_set_arrival` (the arrival the user dragged in B), `link_connect`, `link_cancel`, ...); A's draft waits among `EditorSession::parked` while B is open and nothing is written until Save. `src/exit_arrival.rs`: the arrival point next to an exit (`arrival_next_to`, `arrival_error`) and the exit a click makes (`exit_at_click`).
- `src/editor_inbound.rs`: the arrivals of other rooms' exits that lead into the room being edited (`inbound_arrivals`, `move_inbound_arrival`: parks and dirties the other room's draft, leaves this room alone). `src/editor_selection.rs`: the canvas `Selection` enum (with `Inbound` and `LinkArrival`, which `edits_current_room` says do not edit this room); `EditorSession::drag_selection` uses that for the unsaved flag.
- `src/editor_history.rs`: snapshot undo/redo for the room draft (limit 100; a drag is one step) and the Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y key mapping.
- `src/editor_terrain.rs`: pure terrain edits on the room draft (add, move, resize, delete, hit tests, handles), validated as a whole.
- `src/editor_tools.rs`: the editor tool enum (Select, Place, Terrain, Erase), its key map, and the terrain tool's selection and drag state.
- `src/editor_view.rs`: pure editor canvas math: zoom/pan transform, grid snapping, window regions, footer wrapping.
- `src/room_size.rs`: validated room bounds/floor edits for the editor.
- `src/definition_editor.rs` (+ `definition_editor/`): pure entity/behavior edits, save planning, and staged file writes. The parent re-exports every public item, so `hollow_steps::definition_editor::*` paths are unchanged. `draft.rs`: `DefinitionDraft`, `load` and read-only queries; `plan.rs`: `plan`, `prepare`, `commit`, `SavePlan`, snapshot checks, `write_files`, `serialize`; `entities.rs`: entity and behavior edits, `entity_from_fields`, `validate_entity_field`; `states.rs`: state edits, `ActionInput`, `TransitionInput`, `StateInputs`, `remove_row`, `move_row`.
- `src/search.rs`: pure editor list filtering and stable selection.

Composition and front end:

- `src/body_draw.rs`: Macroquad adapter: `draw_body` (ordered body parts for enemies, bosses and the player), the shared `body_tint` (hurt flash, warning tint, fade), legacy enemy boxes, and debug overlays.
- `src/game/pause_link.rs`: how `GameState` owns the `PauseMenu`: `press_escape` (closes the inventory first, else toggles the menu or backs out of Settings; ignored in the editor), `is_paused` (inventory or menu; `update` and `shake_offset` use it, the editor stops the world separately), and the `pause_step` / `pause_step_slot` / `pause_hover*` / `pause_activate*` accessors `main.rs` drives the menu through (Reset keybinds is done in them, only `Exit` is left for the front end); `toggle_inventory` is ignored while the menu is open. Tests in `src/game/pause_tests.rs`.
- `src/game/keybind_link.rs`: how `GameState` owns the `Keybinds`: `keybinds`, `use_keybinds_file` (loads the saved file, saves every later change there; no file path until it is called, so tests never write a config folder), `pause_bind_key` / `pause_clear_key` (the key pressed while a keybind row waits), `reset_keybinds` (deletes the file) and `finish_pause_press`. Tests in `src/game/keybind_tests.rs`.
- `src/charm_menu.rs`: the inventory's Charms section as pure state: `CharmMenu` (the cursor over the owned charms, which wraps around the ends; the top of the scrolling window; the last refusal message; `step`, `select`, `toggle` -> `Toggle`), `owned_charms`, `max_mana_after` (the same toggle tried on a copy of the `CharmState`) and `mana_text`. Sub-module `layout.rs`: the layout the drawing and the mouse hit test share (`layout` for the whole inventory panel, which is as tall as its content, `visible_rows` for how many charm rows fit the view, `charm_at`, `row_columns`; only the virtual view size goes in). Sub-module `text.rs`: `fit_line` and `wrap_lines` (the caller passes the measuring function). Tests in `src/charm_menu/tests.rs` and `src/charm_menu/text/tests.rs`.
- `src/game/charm_link.rs`: how `GameState` owns the charms: the `CharmCatalog` and `CharmState` accessors, `base_max_mana` / `base_max_health` (the numbers without charms, from the `PlayerDraft`), `toggle_charm` (equip or take off, refused when over the mana budget), the inventory list's methods (`charm_step`, `charm_hover`, `charm_press`, `charm_click`, `inventory_layout`, `charm_mana_preview`; they do nothing while the inventory is closed) and `apply_stats`, the one function that puts the draft's base numbers plus the equipped charms into effect on the player, the vitals and the enemies (new game, F5, F4, editor sync, Discard, vitals reload and equipping all end in it). Tests in `src/game/charm_tests.rs` and `src/game/charm_menu_tests.rs`.
- `src/game/vitals_link.rs`: how `GameState` drives the vitals: input gating while a heal runs, healing and regain copied into `Health`, and the F4 / F5 accessors (`vitals`, `set_vitals_config`, `save_vitals`).
- `src/game/player_link.rs`: how `GameState` owns the one `PlayerDraft` (stats and vitals) that F4 and the editor's Player panel share: `player_draft`, `set_tuning_field`, `spawn_checks`, `save_tuning`, and `sync_player_from_editor` (copies the session's draft into the game each editor frame and applies a valid one to the player and enemies without resetting them). `save_player` checks the stats, the vitals and the look before writing `player.ron`, then `vitals.ron` (only if the vitals changed) and `player_body.ron` (only if the look changed); `discard_player_edits` puts the game, the session and the look back to what is saved.
- `src/game/test_room.rs`: Test room: swaps the editor's draft in as the playable room, parks the editor session and player, and restores them (the run's charms too, after it owned the `owned_at_start` ones); the unsaved player stats and charm list are applied first and an unusable player, charm list or look refuses the start (`player_problem`, same rules as Save); `test_player_body` is the unsaved look as a `PlayerBody` for `main.rs` to draw.
- `src/game.rs`: top-level gameplay state and world composition. Add new
  gameplay systems here as separate modules or fields, keeping each system's
  rules testable without a window.
- `src/editor_panel.rs`: egui editor panel frame (`draw_panel`, the Room tools / Entity properties switch).
